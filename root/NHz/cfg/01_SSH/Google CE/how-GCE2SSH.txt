--- how.GoogleCloudEngine-2-SSH

add Public Key to Google Cloud:

Register the key with your Compute Engine instance.

Open and copy the entire text inside 
    "kpTrixie.pub"  =:<sshKey.pub>
using a text editor.

Go to Google Cloud Console > VM instances.

Click your VM instance name and 
    click Edit at the top bar.

Scroll down to SSH Keys, 
    click Add Item, 
    and paste the copied public key.

Click Save at the bottom of the page.
