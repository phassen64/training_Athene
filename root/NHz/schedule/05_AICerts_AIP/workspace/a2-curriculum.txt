AI+ Cloud Practitioner
Program Detailed Curriculum


Executive SummaryThe AI+ Cloud Practitioner certification provides learners with foundational and practical knowledge of cloud
computing, artificial intelligence, machine learning, data engineering, generative AI, and cloud-based AI solutions. The
course covers cloud platforms, AI workloads, LLM deployment, RAG systems, fine-tuning, Agentic AI, evaluation,
security, and responsible AI practices. Learners gain hands-on experience through use cases, case studies, and a
capstone project focused on designing and deploying scalable AI solutions using cloud technologies. This certification
prepares professionals to understand modern AI ecosystems and apply cloud-enabled AI capabilities across diverse
business and industry environments.


Prerequisites :
Cloud Computing Awareness: Understand basic cloud concepts and services.
AI Fundamentals Knowledge: Familiarity with artificial intelligence concepts and applications.
Programming Basics: Understand fundamental programming logic and concepts.
Data Understanding Skills: Read and interpret basic data concepts.
Technical Learning Mindset: Explore AI, cloud, and emerging technologies
