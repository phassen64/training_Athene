>   Question 24:  
	Which of the following ethical principles is directly 
        undermined by so-called "black-box" machine learning models?
<   Transparency || Explainability


$<  Why Transparency is Undermined
    The Black-Box Problem: 
        Complex machine learning models 
        (like deep neural networks or ensemble models) 
        transform input data into predictions through millions 
        or billions of parameters. 
    Because these internal decision-making paths are opaque, 
    humans cannot easily see or comprehend
    how or why a specific prediction was reached.
    Impact on Accountability & Fairness: 
        Without transparency, 
    it is extremely difficult to verify whether 
        a model is making decisions based on legitimate 
        factors or discriminatory biases, 
    making it hard to hold the system or its creators accountable for harmful errors.
